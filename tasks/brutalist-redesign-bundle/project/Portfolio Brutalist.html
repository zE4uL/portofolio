§04 / Process * Four moves, in sequence [C] Engineers in the room.
P.01

Listen — to the business and the user.

Half my job is translating B2B revenue goals into B2C delight. I don't sketch a screen until I can name the metric it moves and the person it serves — in one sentence.

+
P.02

System — before I make screens.

Tokens, themes, primitives. I'd rather spend a week on the design system than three months patching inconsistencies. Float and the BlueStacks system both started as one disciplined Friday.

+
P.03

Prototype — vibe-coded, not vibes-only.

I prototype in code with AI agents and plugins as my second pair of hands. Real interactions, real tokens. The prototype is the spec — ask anyone who saw the scroll-wheel weapon switch.

+
P.04

Ship, then sharpen — with the data.

I sit close to engineering and analytics. We ship, we measure, we cut what didn't earn its keep. The first version isn't the last good one — it's the start of the conversation.
