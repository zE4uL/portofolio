# 6labs.ai — AI Gameplay Analytics Platform

**Company:** BlueStacks / now.gg
**Years:** 2025–present (current work)
**Role:** Product Designer (lead designer on the platform)
**Tier:** 1 — headline case study, AI flagship

---

## What it is (public context)
6Labs is referenced inside now.gg's developer documentation as a module within the nowStudio ecosystem. Publicly, BlueStacks runs **AI Highlights** — automated gameplay capture that produces curated highlight clips and snapshots from real player sessions. 6labs.ai sits a layer above this: an AI platform trained on the gameplay video data produced by millions of real players inside the BlueStacks App Player, surfacing developer-grade analytics that previously required manually watching playtest recordings.

## My role / contribution
- Lead designer for the 6labs.ai platform (product surface for game devs).
- Designed the IA, query/analysis flows, and "how do devs ask the AI questions about their players" experience.
- Working in an **AI-native workflow**:
  - Using Claude Code to iterate on production code, push the result into Figma, edit there, push back into the codebase — round-trip Figma↔code as part of normal design work.
  - Built **custom Claude Code skills and plugins** to speed up common design+code tasks for the team.
  - Run **internal AI workshops** to upskill the broader BlueStacks/now.gg team on AI-native workflows; raising AI awareness across the company.
