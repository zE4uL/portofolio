# BlueStacks Moments — Gameplay Capture

**Company:** BlueStacks
**Years:** ~2022
**Role:** Designer (feature work)
**Tier:** 3 — fold into AI Highlights case study

---

## What it is (public context)
Press CTRL+M during gameplay to capture the **last 60 seconds** as a high-quality clip. Auto-uploads to now.gg cloud + saves locally. Built-in editor. Sharing via copy-link.

## My role / contribution
Feature-level work on Moments — capture flow, gallery, editor entry points, sharing.

## Why fold into AI Highlights
Moments is the **manual** version of what AI Highlights now does **automatically**. The senior story is the evolution: "we shipped Moments as user-initiated capture, observed which moments people captured, then built AI Highlights to surface them automatically." That's a far stronger product narrative than treating them as separate features.
