## What it is (public context)
nowStudio is now.gg's developer platform for publishing, monetizing, and scaling mobile games. It evolved into a **multi-store publishing system**: from one upload, a game can be published to:
- **PC** (BlueStacks app player)
- **Mac**
- **Cloud / browser** (now.gg)
- **LINE** (Japanese super-app)
- **Telegram** (NowStore — recent expansion, payments via TON)
- **Mobile** (BlueStacks mobile app)

Devs manage all channels from one interface. Includes real-time session tracking, growth analytics, test-link generation, and pre-launch feedback collection. Powered by nowSDK — described publicly as "the third largest distribution after Apple and Google."
