# ML-Driven Discovery + Ad Placements + AI Highlights

**Company:** BlueStacks / now.gg
**Years:** 2021–present
**Role:** Product designer leading AI-centric experimentation
**Tier:** 1 — headline case study, consumer ML/UX

---

## What it is (public context)
Two interlinked AI surfaces inside the BlueStacks App Player and Cloud Player:

1. **ML-driven ad placements & discovery** (BlueStacks App Player + now.gg Cloud Player)
   - ML decides what gets surfaced to which player and when, optimizing simultaneously for revenue (B2B advertiser value) and discovery quality (B2C engagement).
   - Bridges B2B monetization needs with high-quality B2C experience — a rare combo to design for.

2. **AI Highlights** (publicly launched on BlueStacks 5)
   - AI watches gameplay and automatically captures epic wins, memorable plays, standout moments.
   - User chooses Snapshot (lightweight summary) or Video (high-quality clips).
   - Surfaced in Media Gallery → AI Highlights → Recent Activity.
   - Available for supported games; minimum 45 seconds of footage.
