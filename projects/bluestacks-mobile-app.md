# BlueStacks Mobile App + Cross-Platform Payments

**Company:** BlueStacks / now.gg
**Years:** ~2023–present
**Role:** Product designer
**Tier:** 2 — supporting case study, craft + commerce

---

## What it is
A mobile app for BlueStacks that:
- Surfaces games with deals + offers
- Integrates with **nowSDK** so users get **better in-game discounts** than they would on Play Store, and **devs keep more revenue** than the Play Store cut
- Powered by nowStudio (the publishing backend)

Tied to a **cross-platform payments experience** that you designed to run consistently across:
- BlueStacks App Player (Windows / Mac)
- now.gg Cloud Player (browser)
- BlueStacks mobile app

## My role / contribution
- Mobile app product design (browse, deals, redemption, account).
- Designed the unified payments experience across all three surfaces — single mental model for users, single integration for devs.
- Worked with nowStudio backend to make sure devs publish once and get payments everywhere.

## Why it matters for senior/lead positioning
- **Cross-platform consistency** is a hard, senior-level design problem — most candidates only ship on one platform.
- Commerce / payments work signals you can design for **money and trust**, not just engagement — directly relevant to fintech (Razorpay, CRED, Slice, Jupiter, PhonePe, Zerodha, Groww).
- "Devs win, users win" framing is exactly the marketplace-design narrative super-apps love.
